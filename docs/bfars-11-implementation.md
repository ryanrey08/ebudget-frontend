# BFARS 11 (Statement of Approved Budget, Utilizations, Disbursements and Balances for Trust Receipts): implementation notes

## What was built
- **`/bfars-reporting/bfars-11`**: the dashboard from "eBudget BFARS 11 Reporting Dashboard". Fiscal Year, Quarter, Department/Office, Trust Receipt Fund and UACS (Major) filters with Generate Report; Total Approved Budget, Total Utilizations, Total Disbursements, Unutilized Balance and Cash Balance cards (share of the approved budget with a bar); "Monthly Utilization vs. Disbursements" grouped bars for the quarter's months; the "Trust Receipts by Fund/Activity (Utilizations)" donut; the Trust Receipt Details table (search, pagination, TOTAL row) with Export to Excel, Export to PDF and Print. A "Report Generator" button opens the generator.
- **`/bfars-reporting/bfars-11/generate`**: Report Parameters, Group by (Trust Receipt Fund / Fund / Activity), Display Options (Include Summary Page, Include Detailed Transactions, Show Comparative (Previous Quarter), Include Percentage of Utilization, Include Signatories Page), Output Format (PDF / XLSX / XLS) and the live A4-landscape preview (page navigation, zoom, download, print, full screen), as in BFARS 4–10. "Back to Dashboard" returns to the dashboard.
- The printed report follows "eBudget BFARS 11 Report Dashboard": "BFARS Form 11 / Annex K" header, title, "For the Quarter Ending March 31, 2026 (Fiscal Year 2026)", parameter block, the statement with TOTAL, the reconciliation line from the BFARS 11 sample sheet, both charts (inline SVG) and Prepared by / Reviewed by / Certified Correct, all on page 1. The comparative statement and the monthly breakdown follow on their own pages when selected.
- **Redux**: `sabudbtrSlice` (registered in `store/index.ts`) keeps the applied parameters, Group by, display options and output format, so filters applied on the dashboard open in the generator, generator changes come back to the dashboard, and options survive leaving the page. Report data stays in the RTK Query cache.

## Assumptions
1. **Amounts follow MPC_BFARS11_SABUDBTR_Sample_Q1_2026.** Approved budget ₱120M, utilizations ₱95M, disbursements **₱83M**. Both mockups show ₱85M total disbursements (70.83%) and ₱10M cash balance, but their own rows add to ₱83M (36 + 22 + 12 + 8 + 5), as does the workbook, so the totals are ₱83M / ₱12M cash balance (69.17% / 10.00%). Monthly figures come from the workbook's "Monthly Utilization" sheet (utilizations 28.5 / 30.4 / 36.1M, disbursements 24.9 / 26.56 / 31.54M); the dashboard mockup's bars (30/27, 32/28, 33/30) don't add up to either total.
2. **Formulas** are the workbook's: unutilized balance = approved budget − utilizations; cash balance = utilizations − disbursements; % utilized = utilizations ÷ approved budget. The mockups label the second balance "Cash Balance"; the workbook calls it "Undisbursed Utilizations" and notes it is not a verified bank balance, so the dashboard footnote and reconciliation line say so.
3. **Later quarters**: the approved budget is annual and utilizations/disbursements are year-to-date (as BFARS 6). April–December have no sample data, so each month utilizes a small fraction of the fund's average Q1 month (capped at the approved budget) and disburses at the fund's Q1 rate. FY 2025/2024 are scaled ×0.92 / ×0.85 like the other reports.
4. **Activities and offices** aren't in the references. Each fund is split into one or two illustrative activities with an office (shares add back to the workbook line), used by Group by "Fund / Activity", the Department/Office filter and the monthly breakdown.
5. **"Include Detailed Transactions"** shows the monthly utilizations and disbursements per fund/activity (the workbook's "Monthly Utilization" sheet); the references have no transaction-level list for trust receipts.
6. **UACS (Major)** lists the workbook's five placeholder UACS codes, narrowed to the selected fund. Signatory names come from the app's user records (the mockup's names differ). PDF output opens the print dialog (Save as PDF), as in BFARS 1–10.
